Privacy Policy.

Plain language, no traps. Here is exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you hold over every byte.

Last updated · January 2025

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy describes how Porcupine Snowboard School ("Porcupine", "we", "us") handles personal information across our website pages, the online catalogue, the booking form and the newsletter. By using any of these services you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to put you on a snowboard safely:

  • Booking details: name, phone number, email, preferred date and selected package.
  • Lesson data: progress measurements and checkpoint scores recorded by your coach during lessons.
  • Technical data: browser type, device type and anonymous usage statistics that help us fix what's broken.
  • Newsletter data: your email address, used for exactly one weekly email and nothing else.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to confirm and deliver bookings, personalise lesson plans through the Precision Method, process refunds, and — only if you subscribe — send the Summit Newsletter. We never use your lesson data for advertising, and we never build secret profiles of you. We're too busy waxing boards.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use a minimal set of cookies and your browser's local storage for two purposes: remembering what's in your booking cart between visits, and measuring anonymous page performance. You can clear both at any time from your browser settings; the site will keep working, it will just forget your cart.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. The only third parties involved are our payment processor (which sees your transaction, not your lesson history) and our email provider (which sees your address if you subscribed). Both are contractually bound to the same standards as this policy.

6. Data Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit, access to booking records is limited to staff who need it to do their job, and lesson reports are stored on access-controlled systems. If a security incident ever affects your data, we will notify you within 72 hours — by phone and email, not a vague blog post.

7. Your Rights & Choices

At any time you may request: a copy of everything we hold about you, correction of wrong data, deletion of your account and records, or a pause on all communications. Email privacy@porcupine.school and we will complete the request within 30 days, free of charge.

8. Retention

Booking records are kept for 3 years for refund and safety reasons, then anonymised. Newsletter addresses live until you unsubscribe. Cart data lives in your own browser and dies when you clear it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy in any meaningful way, the "last updated" stamp above changes with it and subscribers get a heads-up in the newsletter. Major changes never apply retroactively to data already collected.

10. Contact Us

Questions, requests or just strong opinions about cookies? Write to privacy@porcupine.school or by post to Porcupine Base Cabin, 12 Summit Road, Frostpeak Resort. We answer everything, usually within two working days.
