The Story

It started with a broken binding and a big idea

❖

In the winter of 2016, two friends watched a beginner get yelled at on a slope — by an instructor. They walked home, sketched a one-page manifesto on a napkin, and Porcupine was born in a garage that smelled of wax and instant coffee.

The manifesto still hangs at our base cabin. Its first line: "No beginner should ever feel small on a mountain." Everything since — the small squads, the numeric progress reports, the $19 entry price — exists to keep that promise.

Today we run five dedicated learning slopes at Frostpeak Resort with 32 internationally certified coaches. The napkin is framed. The yelling? Not on our mountain.

Milestones

  • 2016

    Founded in a garage with one borrowed board, two coaches and the napkin manifesto.

  • 2018

    Precision Method v1 launches: 14 measurable checkpoints per lesson, first of its kind for beginners.

  • 2021

    Coach crew grows to 32, all AASI-certified and first-aid trained. Squads capped at six, forever.

  • 2023

    Graduate No. 4,000 celebrates with the whole school. There was cake. There was a confetti cannon.

  • 2025

    Catalogue 2.0 goes live — lessons, rentals and season passes, all bookable online in under a minute.

The Precision Method

Four steps, zero guesswork

❖
01

Measure

A 20-minute assessment maps your balance, flexibility and fear level into a starter profile — before you ever strap in.

02

Map

Your coach drafts a lesson-by-lesson route across our five learning slopes, each checkpoint timed to your profile.

03

Ride

Small squads, patient coaching, and drills that feel like games. Falling is scheduled, taught, and made fun.

04

Refine

After every session you get a numeric report. Tomorrow's plan is rebuilt around it — that's the whole secret.
